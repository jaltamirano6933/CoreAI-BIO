📐 How is the CoreAI Experimental S-Index calculated? (Formula & Scoring Weights)

The CoreAI Experimental S-Index evaluates dataset FAIRness, metadata completeness, repository interoperability, and citation impact for research datasets:

CoreAI Experimental S-Index = [ (FAIR Score × 0.40) + (Metadata Quality × 0.30) + (Repo Interoperability × 0.20) + (Impact × 0.10) ] / 100
1. FAIR Compliance (40%) Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability evaluation.
2. Metadata Quality (30%) MINiML / SOFT XML schema attribute completeness and machine readability.
3. Repo Interoperability (20%) Open persistent identifier resolution (GEO, DOI, Zenodo).
4. Citation & Impact (10%) PubMed publication linkage, citations, and re-use count signals.

Note: The CoreAI Experimental S-Index is an internal research metric designed to assist in dataset FAIR auditing and compliance tracking. It is not an official NIH policy metric.
